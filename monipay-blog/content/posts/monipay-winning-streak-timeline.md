# From 76th to Top 3: The Monipay Winning Streak Timeline

Building infrastructure that simplifies blockchain payments is a marathon. However, the last few months at Monipay have felt like an absolute sprint. We have been heads down building a decentralized platform that turns everyday conversations into actionable financial intents. 

Today, we want to pause and reflect on the winning streak that has validated our mission to build the invisible wallet. Here is a timeline of our recent milestones and ecosystem growth.

## April 2026: The Climb Begins
**Celo Proof of Ship Ranking: 76th**

In April, we began actively participating in Celo's monthly Proof of Ship leaderboard, competing alongside over 300 exceptional projects. At this stage, our focus was clear: make stablecoin payments as easy as sending a message.

We focused heavily on our [core app infrastructure](https://monipay.xyz), allowing users to claim their usernames and begin experiencing true gasless transfers. Ranking 76th was our baseline. We knew we had to push harder.

## May 2026: The AI Breakthrough
**Celo Proof of Ship Ranking: 59th**
**Winner: AI Track at May Proof of Ship**

May was a turning point. We introduced [@monibot](https://x.com/monibot), our autonomous AI agent. By bridging the gap between social platforms and onchain execution, we proved that crypto does not need to be complicated. 

With MoniBot, users could type a Natural language command directly on X, Telegram, or Discord. The AI agent parses the request and executes the transaction onchain instantly. 

Our approach to integrating AI with Social payments stood out. We won the AI Track out of 32 AI-focused projects, a track that saw over 26,000 transactions over 90 days. [View the announcement](https://x.com/CeloDevs/status/2060451622933962969).

## June 2026: Entering the Top Tier
**Celo Proof of Ship Ranking: 32nd**

Our momentum accelerated in June. By refining our ERC-8004 Agent identity system and ensuring smooth execution across multiple networks, our Proof of Ship ranking jumped to 32nd. 

The community started to realize the power of linking wallets to simple usernames. The barrier to entry was dropping, and user adoption was climbing.

## June 18-19, 2026: The Celo Onchain Agents Hackathon
**Placed 3rd (Best Agents on Celo)**

Out of 73 competitive projects, Monipay placed 3rd in the Celo Onchain Agents Hackathon. This was a massive validation of our architecture. 

We showcased our full suite of features: MagicPay, CasualPay, Scheduled payments, and the Gated Access Manager. We proved that [Social payments](https://x.com/monibot) could be trustless, non-custodial, and entirely driven by autonomous AI agents. The judges recognized our ability to turn social feeds into native payment terminals.

## Present Day: Deverse Labs Incubation
**StartupBank 2.0 Acceptance**

To cap off this incredible streak, we were accepted into the Deverse Labs StartupBank 2.0 incubation program. This partnership provides us with critical growth resources and deep distribution access. 

As part of this initiative, we are already live on [MiniPay](https://monipay.xyz/minipay) in Developer beta. Mobile users in emerging markets can now access Monipay directly within the Celo ecosystem without creating new accounts.

## Looking Forward

We are not slowing down. This winning streak is just the beginning of our roadmap to make decentralized finance universally accessible. 

Thank you to everyone who has shipped with us, tested our bots, and supported our vision.
