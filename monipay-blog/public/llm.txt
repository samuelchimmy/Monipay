## About Monipay
Monipay is a non-custodial, AI-powered smart payment platform for stablecoins. It eliminates gas fees, replaces wallet addresses with simple usernames (moniTag™), and enables AI-powered social payments via MoniBot on X, Discord, and Telegram.

Key features:
- Gasless stablecoin payments (USDC, USDT, aUSD)
- moniTag™ username-based payment identity
- MoniBot AI agent for social media payments
- Non-custodial architecture (user holds keys)
- Multi-chain: Base, BSC, Solana, Ink, Celo, Tempo

Technical deep dives, product updates, builder notes, and ecosystem commentary about crypto payments, stablecoins, account abstraction, and agentic commerce.
