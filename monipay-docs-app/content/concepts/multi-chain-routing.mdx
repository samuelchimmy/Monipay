# Cross-chain routing

Monipay simplifies the multi-chain experience by automatically selecting the best path for your payment.

## Routing Logic

When you send a payment to a MoniTag, the app evaluates:
- **Sender Balance:** Which chains have sufficient funds?
- **Recipient Support:** Which chains has the recipient initialized?
- **Sponsor Capacity:** Which chain has the most reliable gas sponsorship at this moment?

Currently, Monipay prioritizes same-chain sends for speed and reliability, but background cross-chain routing is being developed to move funds across networks seamlessly.
