# Reserved usernames

To prevent impersonation and ensure brand integrity, Monipay maintains a blocklist of ~150 reserved usernames.

## Protected Categories

- **Monipay Brand:** `monipay`, `monibot`, `monitag`, `magicpay`.
- **System Roles:** `admin`, `support`, `root`, `moderator`, `official`.
- **Common Service Terms:** `payment`, `bank`, `wallet`, `claim`, `checkout`.
- **Exchanges & Wallets:** `binance`, `coinbase`, `phantom`, `metamask`.

## Policy

Reserved usernames are blocked at the registration level. If you believe you have a legitimate claim to a reserved name (e.g., you own the trademark), please contact [support@monipay.xyz](mailto:support@monipay.xyz).
